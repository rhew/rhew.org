This is the renovation in compressed form: months of work reduced to a minute and a half of walls, windows, rooflines, trim, and a whole lot of things slowly becoming a house again.

The time-lapse below covers progress from March through August 2026. That stretch was the part where the house moved from the place we had been living in toward the renovated version we had been imagining: opened up, rebuilt, cleaned up, and slowly ready for normal life again.

There is still work left after the end of the video. Painting, lighting, ceiling fans, and other finishing details are still on the list. Construction is scheduled to wrap up in October 2026, assuming the universe continues to cooperate in the limited and conditional way it sometimes does.

The fun thing about a time-lapse is that it hides all of the waiting. Permits, scheduling, deliveries, inspections, weather, decisions, revisions, and the thousand small dependencies all disappear. What remains is the satisfying version: the house simply changes shape.

That is not how it felt in real time.

But it is nice to have the satisfying version too.

The video stops in August because the time-lapse camera batteries died. So this is less "final progress report" and more "last surviving footage."
