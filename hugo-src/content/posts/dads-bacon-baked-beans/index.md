Dad doesn't really have a written recipe for baked beans. He has always made them by feel, so the amounts here are ranges on purpose. Taste as you go, cover the whole thing with bacon, and bake it longer than you think.

## Ingredients

- 2 large cans pork and beans (about 28 oz each)
- ¼ to ⅓ cup molasses
- ¼ to ⅓ cup ketchup
- Up to ¼ cup yellow mustard
- 1 onion, cut into wedges
- 1 bell pepper, cut into chunks
- Bacon slices, enough to cover the top, cut crosswise into thirds or quarters

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Pour the pork and beans into a large baking dish. Add the molasses, ketchup, and mustard, then stir until everything is well combined.
3. Space the onion wedges and pepper chunks throughout the beans.
4. Arrange the bacon pieces over the beans in a single layer, covering the whole top.
5. Bake uncovered for at least 1½ hours. They are ready when the bacon is browned and the vegetables nestled in the beans are soft and almost caramelized.

---

The important part is patience. If you look at them after an hour and wonder whether they have cooked long enough, they probably haven't.
